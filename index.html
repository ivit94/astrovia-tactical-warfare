Ability scores

Official rule: every player may choose either Standard Array or dice rolling when creating a commander.

STEP 02 / 07
Both methods are permitted. Choose the method you prefer for this commander. Standard Array uses 15, 14, 13, 12, 10 and 8; rolling uses six rolls of 4d6, dropping the lowest die each time.
Roll 4d6 six times, discard the lowest die from each roll, then assign the six totals to your abilities in any order. You may roll a new set before assigning scores.
Ability scores are for the commander's personal character sheet. They do not automatically replace squad statistics or weapon attack bonuses.

Universal feats
These feats are available across military factions. Feat purchase costs unlock the ability; they do not include additional costs such as purchasing the actual mech unit. Artillery Support and Air Strike Support each have uses equal to your proficiency bonus, up to three uses per battle or deployment.
Your commander begins with 10,000 Credits. Select Buy on a feat to unlock it.
